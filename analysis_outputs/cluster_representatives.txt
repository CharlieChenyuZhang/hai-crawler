Cluster 0 examples:
  1) Write a letter to your younger self, talking about mental health. What would you want to tell them?
  2) Write a letter to your younger self. What advice would you give him or her?

Cluster 1 examples:
  1) Who do you most admire? Why?
  2) Close your eyes and imagine a peaceful, calm place. Where is that place?

Cluster 2 examples:
  1) What am I looking forward to today?
  2) What’s one moment from today (or yesterday) that made you smile?

Cluster 3 examples:
  1) How do you think others see you? Why?
  2) Think about the last time you had a mental spiral. What caused it, and how did you get out of it?

Cluster 4 examples:
  1) When do you feel most alive?
  2) Do you have a hobby? How do you feel when you're engaged with it?

Cluster 5 examples:
  1) Name three beautiful things that you heard today.
  2) Name three things that challenged you today.
